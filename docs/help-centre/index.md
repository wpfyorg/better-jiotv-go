# JioTV Help Centre

JioTV runs a TV server on your computer, router or home server. You watch live channels in a browser, check schedules, and use playlist links with an IPTV player. Supported accounts can also browse movies and shows.

**New here?** Follow [Getting Started in 5 Minutes](../getting-started-in-5-minutes/index.md). It explains the difference between the server's admin password and your TV account, and gets you to the channel list.

The guide describes the current **Rust server and Svelte web interface**. Some channel availability, playback, Extras content and DRM depend on your account, your browser and the content provider.

> Your playlist link carries access to the server. Treat it like a password. Never share your data directory, session cookies, OTP codes, or diagnostic logs containing tokens publicly.
